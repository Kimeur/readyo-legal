Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Applies to the Readyo app for iPhone, version 1.0 and later.

In short
  • Readyo is a practice tool: an AI interviewer asks you questions out loud and you answer by voice.
  • You don't create an account with an email or a password. The app signs you in with an anonymous ID.
  • We don't keep your voice recordings. Each answer is sent to Groq to be turned into text, and we don't store the audio. The interviewer's voice is generated on your iPhone.
  • Your transcripts and scores are kept until you delete them in the app: Profile → Delete my data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no camera, and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible for your data

Readyo is published by Marouane Gazouzi, an individual developer (the "controller" of your data under the GDPR). Contact: aveniamarouanemohamed@gmail.com.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. We never use the camera. We don't infer emotions, confidence or personality from your voice: scores are based on the words of your answers and their timing only.

AI processing

Before every practice interview, the app tells you that the interviewer is an AI, not a real person, and asks for your agreement before your voice or text is sent to our AI partners. Scores are AI estimates for practice. They are not hiring decisions, and Readyo never shares them with employers.

Transfers outside your country

Our database is hosted in the European Union. Some partners (Groq, RevenueCat) process data in the United States. Where the GDPR applies, these transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract: to provide the practice interviews and reports you ask for, and the purchases you make.
  • Consent: to send your voice and answers to our AI partners. You give it on the "Before you start" screen and can withdraw it at any time by not starting an interview and deleting your data.
  • Legal obligation: to keep purchase records for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Legitimate interest: to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, using technical logs that contain IDs and timings only, never transcripts or audio.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No camera, no face or emotion analysis, no voiceprints or voice cloning.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my data and confirm. This permanently deletes your interviews, transcripts, reports and your anonymous account from our database. It cannot be undone.

Deleting your data does not cancel a Pro subscription: Apple keeps billing until you cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required by law; write to us if you want RevenueCat's record removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw your consent. Because your account is anonymous, the fastest way to delete everything is in the app. For other requests, email us. Because we don't know who you are, we may need details from the app (such as the date of an interview) to find your data. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Readyo is for job seekers and is not directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has used Readyo, contact us and we will delete the data.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted, each user can read only their own data, and API keys never ship inside the app.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app when a change affects how your voice or answers are processed.
