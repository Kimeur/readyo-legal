Readyo

The honest interview coach. Practice out loud, in your language.

Readyo is an iPhone app for job-interview practice. An AI interviewer asks you questions out loud, you answer by voice, and you get a score, feedback and an improved answer for each question. Practice only: Readyo never helps during a real interview.
