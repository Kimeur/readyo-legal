What Readyo is

Readyo is a job-interview practice tool. An AI interviewer asks you questions out loud, you answer by voice, and the app gives you a score, feedback and an improved version of each answer.

  • Practice only. You must not use Readyo to get help during a real interview or any real assessment, or to deceive an employer.
  • AI, not a person. The interviewer is generated by AI. The app says so before every interview and at the start of it.
  • Estimates, not predictions. Scores and feedback are AI estimates to help you practise. They can be wrong. They are not a prediction of any hiring decision and are not career, legal, medical or psychological advice. We don't guarantee any interview result or job offer.
  • Improved answers are built from what you said. Check them and replace anything in [brackets] with your own true facts before using them.
